Privacy Policy

How Spendly handles your information.

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account we collect your name, email address, and password. When you use Spendly we store the expenses you record, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to run Spendly for you: to sign you in, to save and display your expenses, and to produce the summaries and filters you ask for. We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on the servers that host Spendly and is kept for as long as your account is active. Passwords are stored in hashed form rather than as plain text. While we take reasonable steps to protect your data, no system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You may request deletion of your account and data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser may contact Google's servers when you visit the site. We do not share your expense data with any other third party, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or want to request access to or deletion of your data, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
